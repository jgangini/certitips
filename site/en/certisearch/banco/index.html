You can learn the process without a client or confirmed quantities. When preparing a proposal, record what is known and mark unknown information as pending; do not invent figures or choose a service before comparing alternatives.

Outcome and user defined, with a measure of success.

Initiative identified as new, migration or modernization.

Facts separated from assumptions; relevant gaps recorded.

What result, user and success measure warrant this case and what remains outside the first proposal?

What regions will have data and users, what network routes are allowed and what volume will cross clouds or data centers?

What roles, sensitive data, audit, encryption, secrets and access restrictions should be shown?

What availability, RTO, RPO, maintenance window and care responsibility require each component?

What budget, currency, monthly horizon, environments and growth can the client review, and what SKU, quantities and monthly hours support the complementary resources?

Who will operate every component and with what coverage, alarms, logs and skills?

Link needs to capabilities and candidate alternatives.

Represent components, flows, owners and decisions.

After identifying needs and comparing alternatives, the measurements depend on the workload. Only where relevant, collect data for the following questions according to the data, analytics, container, API or artificial intelligence workload.

What's the daily volume, file size, growth and maximum process window?

How many authors and consumers will have, how many will coincide at peak hour and what actions will they conduct?

How much CPU and memory do pods request and consume under normal and peak load, including platform components, and what OCPU and RAM per worker meet that demand?

How many requests monthly and by second at peak are expected, with what size and latency of response?

How many transactions, input and output tokens, and concurrent requests are expected, and what is their size distribution?

Each quantity has a unit, period and source.

Components without a verifiable rate are shown without a price.

Target and transition costs are separate, with uncertainty visible.

What type and quantity of CPU, memory per worker, number of workers and instances does each workload require, how many hours per month will they run, and what verified monthly total of AIDP units applies?

Building the compute stage by environment and type of work.

Record minimums, maximums and idle time per workload; verify the available configuration and measure with representative data. For heterogeneous topologies, request the monthly total of AIDP units verified using the official conversion, including catalog, Spark and AI Compute; do not multiply it by hours again or add it to a profile covering the same resources.
