Modeling Data and Design

They represent entities, relationships, validity and meaning without confusing a scheme with semantics.

At Horizonte, a person may have several contracts, a contract may produce several bills and a consent may change with time. If the model ignores these differences, a report can double amounts and an assistant can assign an authorisation to the wrong purpose.

Key Concepts

A data model It represents a part of the business so that we can communicate and work with her. Select relevant entities, attributes, relationships and restrictions. It does not reproduce the whole reality: it makes clear the decisions that matter for a use. "Customer" requires an agreement as to whether it means person, organisation, commercial account or other entity.

The cardinality indicates how many events can be related. A client can have many contracts. Each example contract belongs to a client. That rule should be validated with the business: if there are joint headlines, the model needs another relationship. Draw first an foreign key without agreeing to the rule can consolidate an incorrect assumption.

The granularity Answer what a row represents. A row for an invoice and a row for an invoice line are not interchangeable. By combining sets with different granularities, we have to preserve relations and amounts correctly. A valid SQL result can be semantically wrong.

From Meaning to Physical Model

The model conceptual allows to discuss entities and rules without relying on a database. The logical requires attributes, identifiers, relationships and restrictions. The physical concrete tables, appropriate types, indexes and structures for the chosen engine. To move from one view to another requires decisions. That's not just translating names.

A technical key identifies a registry a business key can identify an entity in a context. A client number of two different systems can match without referring to the same person. That's why the mapping between identifiers should maintain its origin. Reducibility and identity decisions are deeply focused on master data.

The time's modeling as well. Horizonte record purpose, state and valid consent. A currently boolean indicator does not explain what authorization existed at an earlier date. The model defines what history and how it's been interpreted. The applicable retention shall be agreed with the competent officials.

Modeling and Semantics in Oracle

In Oracle AI Data Platform and Oracle Autonomous AI Lakehouse, schemes, tables and views allow to materialise models and data products. The catalogue describes the assets but to discover their columns does not automatically decide the granularity and definition of a metric. The team keeps that correspondence between business model and published structures.

One semantic layer expressed terms, measures and relations for consistent consumption. One ontology makes explicit entities and relations of a domain; can provide a context for analytics and agents. He's no substitute for his physical design. Nor does it guarantee that an inference of AI coincides with the definition approved by the organisation.

In Oracle Analytics Cloud, use Semantic Modeler to connect tables with terms and measures of the business. Define physical bonds by their keys, represents Customer and Contract by logical relations and cardinality and publish a model that analysts can re-use. Oracle AI Data Platform and Oracle Autonomous AI Lakehouse provide data prepared for this consumption.

To measure "active customers," select existing contracts at cut date and count different client identifiers. C7 contributes a client even if they have several contracts currently in place. The owner approves the definition. model sets up aggregation of the measure and contrasts with known cases. At Oracle Autonomous AI Database, Data Analysis allows to organize dimensions, hierarchies and measures at analytic views. That's how you apply the DAMA modeling: you preserve the agreed meaning from the entities up to the published indicator.

Explanated example

Horizonte has an invoice of 100 currency units with two lines: 60 per service and 40 per installation. If the computer links the header with the lines and adds the total header, get 200. The consultation correctly implements the union. The error is to add a measure of another granularity.

The solution is to add line amounts or first add to the invoice level before combining with other sets. The steward document the metric. The modeling confirms the relationship; The technical team implements a consistent view of Oracle Autonomous AI Lakehouse's product. The case shows why it isn't enough to ask assistant to "compute income" without defining level, period and rule.

Common

  • To design only from available columns. Business rules are omitted that today's columns may not represent.
  • Using the name as an unambiguous identifier. Different people can share and a person can change them.
  • To believe that more context removes all ambiguity. The terms, relations and inferences proposed require accountability and review.

Decision exercise

Horizonte needs to answer if the client authorized commercial communications at the date of a campaign. Just keeps a column consentimiento_actual. That's enough to answer? What should model represent?

Solution

That's not good enough. The model needs to link the subject with the purpose and its state over time, including the event or evidence that supports the change. It should allow an interpretation of its validity by the date referred to. The policy defines conservation and uses. store more default history does not replace that decision.
